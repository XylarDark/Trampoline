# Trampoline Strategy

Working name. Trampoline is a dual-rebuild readiness passport: a router and a progress record, not a service provider.

## Target problem

People who have to turn around their health and their income at the same time get split across apps: wellness tools assume income is stable, career tools treat health as a sidebar. Nobody on either side can tell when the person can actually handle the next load — a course, a placement, a shift.

## Our approach

We hold one staged record of demonstrated readiness. Established services perform the checks and we store their attestations; levels, gates, expiry, and handoffs are ours. We never deliver care, training, or job inventory ourselves.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** The person rebuilding both at once - they're hiring Trampoline to prove, in a way a school or employer will accept, that they are ready for the next step right now.

**Secondary:** The attestor and the gatekeeper - a clinic, gym, counselor, school, agency, or employer hiring Trampoline to receive staged, qualified referrals instead of unscreened walk-ins.

## Key metrics

- **Gated seats filled** - placements taken through a Level 2/3 gate; the primary proof the passport reduced risk.
- **Attestation currency** - share of active users whose checks are unexpired; measures whether the record stays trustworthy.
- **90-day hold rate** - people still in school or work at 90 days without dropping the health floor.
- **Attestor activity** - partner orgs issuing at least one check per month.

## Tracks

### Passport and levels

The user-facing track: dual intake, the four check types, level calculation, and the evidence locker.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Levels are bundles of passed checks, so readiness is demonstrated rather than claimed.

### Attestor and gate network

Partner-facing track: attestors issue and revoke checks; schools and employers publish requirements as gates.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Incumbents keep delivery and get a qualified funnel; we own only the connective tissue.

### Trust surface

Share views, expiry and decay, published rubrics, minimum-necessary disclosure.

_Why it serves the approach:_ A credential is worthless if a gatekeeper cannot trust it or if it leaks health detail.

## Not working on

- Our own clinic, gym network, class schedule, calorie tracker, EMR, LMS content, or resume rewriter.
- Scraped mass job inventory to compete with Indeed.
- Any chatbot that issues medical or mental-health clearance.
- Consumer-only subscription as the primary business model.

## Marketing

**One-liner:** A readiness passport that proves you can handle the next step, and unlocks the seat when you can.

**Key message:** Health and work readiness on one record. Partners perform the checks; the passport carries the proof, the restrictions, and the expiry date.
